🌟 Master Erik's Journey

1985

Born in the Forest

Erik was born in a cabin deep in Swedish forests, where ancient pines whispered their secrets to him from birth.

1995

First Carving

At age 10, Erik carved his first piece - a small raven from fallen birch. His grandfather recognized the gift immediately.

2003

Master's Apprentice

Began 7-year apprenticeship with Master Björn the Elder, learning secrets of Nordic wood carving passed down for generations.

2010

Master Craftsman

Completed his masterwork - a life-sized Nordic wolf that seemed to breathe with forest spirit. Achieved Master status.

2015

International Recognition

Won the Golden Chisel award at the World Nordic Craft Festival. His work began reaching collectors worldwide.

2024

Dostik Partnership

Joined DostanWebCSS as featured master artisan, bringing authentic Nordic wood carving to digital collectors.
